Who we are

A startup made by young people who want robotics to feel possible.

We are not pretending to be a huge company. We are a small, hungry, young team that just finished school and decided to keep building. Kinectiks exists because we believe robotics should not only live in expensive labs. It should be in classrooms, workshops, farms, community spaces, and real businesses that need motion, sensing, and smart control.

Our advantage is speed, curiosity, and the courage to learn in public. We prototype, test, document, improve, and bring people into the process.

Inside the lab

Robotics is not one skill. It is a team of skills moving together.

Kinectiks brings together mechanics, electronics, software, design, documentation, and teaching. The goal is simple: turn an idea into something that can move, sense, respond, and teach.

Mechanical design Frames, joints, mounts, gears, wheels, levers, assemblies
Electronics Motors, drivers, batteries, sensors, wiring, controllers
Software Embedded code, control logic, web dashboards, data display
Education Workshops, lesson flow, demos, student challenges, mentorship
Documentation Build notes, photos, diagrams, parts lists, testing results

How we work

A clear path from rough idea to working demo.

Discover

Understand the problem

We ask what needs to move, what must be sensed, who will use it, and what success looks like.

Sketch

Map the system

We plan the parts: structure, motors, sensors, controller, power, software, and user flow.

Prototype

Build the first version

We make a practical demo that proves the idea can move, react, or automate the task.

Improve

Test and refine

We watch what fails, fix the weak points, and prepare the next version with better evidence.

FAQ

Quick answers.

Are you already a full robotics company?

We are an early startup. That means we are building our portfolio, testing ideas, and looking for serious partners while we grow.

Can Kinectiks work with schools?

Yes. School demos, robotics clubs, workshops, and student build challenges are a big part of our mission.

Can you build a custom robot?

We can help with concept development and prototypes. If the project is large, we will be honest about what is possible and what support is needed.

How can someone support the startup?

You can offer mentorship, tools, components, workspace, sponsorship, introductions, or a real project brief for us to explore.
